Embedded Agent Skills and Research Agents
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Agentic Orchestrator embeds and redistributes adapted skill instructions and
research-agent instructions. Commit-pinned sources, attributions, and the
applicable license text are embedded beside the affected assets; skill
companion files are also reconciled into each Agentic Orchestrator state
directory.

anthropics/skills (https://github.com/anthropics/skills)

    skills/frontend-design is adapted from Anthropic's frontend-design skill,
    originally authored by Keith Lazuka and updated by William Qian.

    Pinned source:
    https://github.com/anthropics/skills/blob/2235be7c60b551f5de82ade908fd3816455afcda/skills/frontend-design/SKILL.md

    Licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0. The complete license and
    attribution are embedded beside the Agentic Orchestrator skill.

mattpocock/skills (https://github.com/mattpocock/skills)

    Copyright (c) 2026 Matt Pocock

    Portions of skills/design, skills/create-roadmap, skills/plan-phase (and
    its format.md), and skills/review-implementation-craft are adapted from
    Matt Pocock's MIT-licensed to-prd, to-issues, and code-review skills.
    Commit-pinned sources, attributions, and the complete MIT license are
    embedded beside each affected Agentic Orchestrator skill.

humanlayer/humanlayer (https://github.com/humanlayer/humanlayer)

    Copyright (c) 2024, humanlayer Authors

    skills/research-codebase and four embedded research agents are adapted
    from HumanLayer's Apache-2.0 research command and agents, originally
    authored by Allison Durham and subsequently maintained by HumanLayer
    contributors.
    skills/build-knowledge-base is informed by the same documentation-only
    methodology and retains limited explanatory language from it. Pinned
    sources and attributions are embedded beside the affected assets.

Acknowledged methodology inspiration (no substantive verbatim overlap found):

obra/superpowers (https://github.com/obra/superpowers)

    Copyright (c) 2025 Jesse Vincent

    Agentic Orchestrator's implementation skill was informed by Superpowers'
    test-driven development, subagent-driven development, and verification-
    before-completion methodology. The acknowledgement and MIT license are
    embedded beside skills/implement.
