# Sources

This playbook is synthesized from the following primary sources and research
organizations.

  - https://fluent2.microsoft.design/color

- IBM Carbon Design System: 2x Grid, typography, color, motion
  - https://carbondesignsystem.com/elements/2x-grid/overview/
  - https://carbondesignsystem.com/elements/typography/overview/
  - https://carbondesignsystem.com/elements/color/overview/
  - https://carbondesignsystem.com/elements/motion/overview/

- GOV.UK Design System: type scale, headings, layout, spacing, colour
  - https://design-system.service.gov.uk/styles/type-scale/
  - https://design-system.service.gov.uk/styles/headings/
  - https://design-system.service.gov.uk/styles/layout/
  - https://design-system.service.gov.uk/styles/spacing/

- Nielsen Norman Group: principles of visual design
  - https://www.nngroup.com/articles/principles-visual-design/
  - Long-running UX research organization founded by Jakob Nielsen and Don Norman.
