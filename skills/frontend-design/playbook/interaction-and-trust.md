# Interaction and Trust

## Navigation Should Match the Task

- Use browse-style navigation for exploration.
- Use linear step-by-step flows for applications, checkouts, setup flows, and
  other guided tasks.
- Keep navigation locations and labels stable across screens.
- On dense products, breadcrumbs, tabs, and local section headers are clearer
  than adding a third global navigation tier.

## Recognition Over Recall

- Keep labels visible.
- Make shortcuts discoverable in context.
- Preserve state and entered data whenever possible.
- Use progressive disclosure to hide rare complexity, not core actions.

## Forms Should Feel Shorter Than They Are

- Prefer single-column layouts for most forms.
- Ask only for information you need now.
- Explain why sensitive information is needed at the point of request.
- Validate at helpful moments: not after every keystroke, not only after
  submission.
- Show both a summary of errors and field-level guidance when the form is long
  or high stakes.

## Feedback Should Match Consequence

- Inline status is best for ongoing background work, autosave, or lightweight
  state changes.
- Toasts or snackbars are useful for temporary acknowledgements and reversible
  actions.
- Blocking modals are for destructive, irreversible, or high-risk moments.
- Success states should tell the user what happened and what to do next.

## Progressive Disclosure Keeps Complexity Humane

- Show the default path first.
- Reveal advanced filters, diagnostics, settings, and edge-case options on
  demand.
- Do not hide essential choices behind "advanced" labels just to make the
  screen look simple.

## Onboarding Should Be Task-First

- Prefer contextual hints, examples, and progressive empty states over long
  tutorials.
- Let users get to first success quickly.
- For irreversible submission flows, provide a review step and a clear
  confirmation state.

## Trust Comes From Clarity

- Be specific about data use, saved progress, waiting states, and failures.
- Error messages should explain what went wrong, how to recover, and what was
  preserved.
- Avoid hostile copy, vague warnings, and unexplained disabled states.
